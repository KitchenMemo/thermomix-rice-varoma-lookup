Quick Reference

  • Best-supported method for rice plus a main: simmering basket in the bowl, Varoma above, using a compatible recipe.
  • Basket limit: 350 g rice max, 1.4 L basket volume, never above the max mark. This limit applies to the basket only.
  • There is no universal water ratio or time. Basket, Varoma container and Rice Cooker mode each hydrate rice differently.
  • Keep some Varoma dish and tray slots uncovered so steam can escape.
  • Cooked rice: cool within ~1 hour, refrigerate, eat within 24 h, reheat once, steaming hot throughout.

FAQ

QuestionShort answer
Can I steam rice and a main together?Yes. Rice in the basket, main in the Varoma, with steam slots left open.
How much rice at once?350 g max in the basket. Other containers need their own validated limit.
Do I have to rinse?Only when your recipe says so. Rinsing alone won't guarantee non-clumpy rice.
What is Varoma?The steaming function. On TM7, choose Steaming mode.
Does TM7 Steaming mode change rice settings?It changes how you select Varoma, not the water ratio or time.
Can I reuse the 350 g limit for a bundt tin?No. It applies to the simmering basket only.
Is there a no-Varoma option?Yes: TM5 basket method for plain white rice, and TM6 Rice Cooker mode.
